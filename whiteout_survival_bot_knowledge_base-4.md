Whiteout Survival Bot Knowledge Base
Purpose
This file is designed as a retrieval knowledge source for a Discord bot, website chatbot, or AI assistant that answers natural-language questions about Whiteout Survival.
It is an original, condensed synthesis of publicly available guides and community knowledge. It does not reproduce full third-party guides verbatim.
Bot Answering Rules
Answer the exact question first.
Distinguish between:
Confirmed mechanic: stated in the game or consistently documented.
Common strategy: broadly used but may vary by generation, stats, or opponent.
Experimental recommendation: should be tested with battle reports.
Ask for these details when they materially affect the answer:
State age or hero generation
Furnace/Fire Crystal level
Available heroes, stars, widgets, and expedition skill levels
Chief gear, charms, hero gear, pets, and research
Whether the user is rally leader, rally joiner, garrison captain, or solo attacker
Event name and whether the question is PvE or PvP
Never claim one troop ratio is universally best.
For combat advice, recommend comparing battle reports and changing one variable at a time.
For current schedules, transfer groups, redeem codes, packs, event scoring tables, or newly released heroes, tell the user to verify the current in-game event page.
Do not confuse:
Exploration skills with Expedition skills
Rally captain heroes with rally joiner lead heroes
Arena lineups with expedition marches
Bear Hunt PvE ratios with PvP ratios
State Transfer power with visible total power
When uncertain, state the uncertainty clearly.

Furnace and building progression
Research
Troop tier and troop quantity
Heroes, stars, skills, and exclusive widgets
Hero gear
Chief gear and Chief charms
Pets
Experts and later-generation systems
Alliance technology, territory, facilities, and leadership
Event resource management
PvP coordination
A player can show high total power while still being weak in combat if too much power comes from troops and not enough comes from permanent combat statistics.
Permanent strength versus temporary power
Permanent strength usually includes:
Research

Hero stars and skills
Hero widgets
Hero gear
Pets

Building and troop-tier unlocks

2. Beginner Progression
Early priorities
Join the strongest active alliance that will accept you.
Keep construction and research running.
Use alliance helps before spending speedups.
Push Furnace requirements efficiently.
Train troops continuously.
Complete Intel missions and daily missions.
Participate in Bear Hunt, Crazy Joe, Alliance Championship, and alliance events.
Save high-value items for scoring events instead of spending them immediately.
Do not overinvest in every hero.
Learn the difference between growth days and combat days.
Best use of gems for most players
Common high-value uses include:
VIP progression or VIP time when it unlocks meaningful daily benefits
Lucky Wheel for a strong generation hero
Extra Arena attempts when rewards justify the cost
Event-specific purchases with unusually high return
Essential teleports or shields during war
Avoid casually spending gems on:
Basic resources
Low-value speedups outside scoring events
Random refreshes with no plan
Healing mistakes that could have been prevented
Resource discipline
Open resource items only when needed. Bagged resources cannot be looted, while exposed city resources may be vulnerable above protected limits.
3. Buildings
Furnace
The Furnace controls the maximum level of many other buildings and unlocks new systems. Its heat also affects survivor well-being.
Before starting a Furnace upgrade:
Confirm all prerequisite buildings.
Request alliance help.
Activate construction buffs if available.
Consider waiting for a construction scoring day.
Avoid using universal speedups unless the reward value is worthwhile.
Embassy
The Embassy affects:
Alliance help capacity
Reinforcement capacity
How many allied troops can support your city
It is important for defense and rally participation.
Command Center
The Command Center increases rally capacity. Battle research can further increase march or rally capacity.
For rally leaders, Command Center progression is a major priority because more troop capacity usually means more total rally damage and durability.
Infirmary
If the Infirmary fills, additional wounded troops may die depending on the battle type. Increase capacity through building upgrades and relevant Growth research.
Before large wars:
Empty the Infirmary.
Avoid unnecessary beast wounds.
Keep healing speedups.
Use alliance batch healing when practical.
Monitor enlistment and recovery systems.
Training camps
Infantry, Lancer, and Marksman camps train their troop types. Upgrade them to unlock higher tiers.
General troop role:
Infantry: front line, absorbs much of the incoming damage
Lancer: mobile middle-line damage and targeting utility
Marksman: high back-line damage
Storehouse
Protects a portion of resources from being looted. Bagged resource items remain safer than opened resources.
Research Center
Research is one of the strongest permanent investments in the game. Long-term combat accounts should not neglect battle research.

5. Troops and Combat Statistics
Four core combat stats
Attack: influences damage dealt
Defense: reduces damage received
Lethality: increases effectiveness of damage
Health: increases troop survivability
A common practical interpretation:
Infantry benefits heavily from Health and Defense.
Marksmen benefit heavily from Attack and Lethality.
Lancers benefit from balanced investment and can influence specific targeting interactions.
Troop tiers
Higher-tier troops usually provide better base statistics. Promote lower-tier troops when event scoring and resources make it efficient.
Do not blindly delete lower-tier troops. Promotion events can convert them while earning points.
March capacity
March capacity is affected by:
Command Center or related buildings
Research
Hero level or hero-related bonuses
Chief level
Pets, skins, or temporary bonuses
Event-specific skills
More capacity is especially important for rally leaders and Bear Hunt.

8. Hero Investment Strategy
Free-to-play
Focus on one strong hero per troop type.
Use Lucky Wheel efficiently for the wheel hero.
Save general Mythic shards for difficult-to-obtain heroes.
Do not spread exclusive widget materials across too many heroes.
Max key expedition skills on heroes used for rallies and garrisons.
Maintain a usable Arena team without sacrificing all combat growth.
Low spender
Choose one or two heroes per generation, not all three.
Prioritize heroes that replace a weak slot or serve multiple modes.
Compare the cost of stars versus widget levels.
Buy packs only when the item bottleneck matches the build plan.
Rally leader
Prioritize:
Highest total expedition stats
Strong rally-captain expedition skills
High widget level
Strong hero gear
March capacity
Current-generation heroes when sufficiently developed
Rally joiner

9. Hero Gear
Hero gear is troop-type specific.
General priority:
Infantry gear: Health and Defense
Marksman gear: Attack and Lethality
Lancer gear: balanced, often after core infantry and marksman sets
For a war account:
Build one excellent Infantry set.
Build one excellent Marksman set.
Build a competitive Lancer set.
Avoid evenly spreading Essence Stones across unused sets.
Move gear between heroes when the mode permits it.
Arena uses five heroes, so multiple functional sets eventually matter.
10. Chief Gear
Chief Gear unlocks around Furnace 22 in commonly documented timelines. It provides troop Attack and Defense.
Piece associations:
Cap and Watch: Lancer
Coat and Pants: Infantry
Ring and Weapon: Marksman
Matching quality sets can provide additional set bonuses.
Common materials:
Hardened Alloy
Polishing Solution
Design Plans
Amber or later-tier materials
General upgrade approach:
Keep pieces reasonably balanced to capture set bonuses.
Give extra priority to Infantry durability and Marksman damage.
Do not create one extremely advanced piece while leaving all other pieces far behind unless a specific event or breakpoint justifies it.
11. Chief Charms
Chief Charms provide major troop combat bonuses and unlock later than Chief Gear.
Common priority:
Marksman offensive charms
Infantry durability charms
Lancer charms after the core sets
This is a meta preference, not a fixed law. Defensive garrison captains may prioritize Infantry more heavily.
Charm materials can include:
Charm Guides
Charm Designs
Charm Secrets
Castle Battle, Frostfire Mine, Foundry-related shops, and other events may supply materials.

13. Experts
Experts are a later system providing specialized bonuses. Because the system changes by state age and update version:
Prioritize permanent combat and development effects.
Avoid upgrading every branch equally.
Match expert bonuses to the account’s role.
Verify whether expert effects are active in the event being discussed.
14. Arena
Arena uses Exploration skills, not Expedition skills.
Basic formation
Usually:
Two durable front-line heroes
Three damage/support heroes behind them
Strategy
Inspect the opposing formation.
Avoid attacking only by displayed power.
Target weak positioning or underdeveloped front lines.
Move burst heroes to attack vulnerable enemy positions.
Use control, healing, and area damage intelligently.
Fight later in the daily window when practical so the final rank is harder to reclaim.
Buy the highest-value long-term materials from the Arena shop.
Common mistakes
Using heroes because they are good in rallies
Ignoring gear distribution
Putting fragile heroes in front
Failing to counter enemy positioning
Spending all attempts early
Fighting a bad matchup because its power is slightly lower
15. Exploration
Exploration is five-hero PvE combat.
Tips:
Use two front-line heroes.
Level the main five heroes efficiently.
Upgrade Exploration skills.
Manually trigger ultimates when timing matters.
Change formation against assassins, area damage, or back-line threats.
Borrow power through gear redistribution when possible.
Retry stages because skill timing and random targeting can change the outcome.
16. Bear Hunt / Bear Trap
Bear Hunt is a frequent alliance PvE rally event. It has no enemy troop composition in the normal PvP sense, so damage optimization differs from war.
Alliance preparation
Fully enhance the trap with donated Explosive Arrowheads.
Place active rally leaders close to the trap.
Set clear rally-launch timing.
Assign joiner heroes.
Remove troops that add little damage if they block higher-damage troops.
Use presets.
Avoid overlapping instructions from multiple leaders.
Rally leader priorities
Best three expedition heroes for PvE damage
Highest march capacity
Strongest troop statistics
High proportion of damage troops
Relevant pet and temporary buffs
Rapid relaunching
Rally joiner priorities
Use the alliance-approved lead hero.
Send the requested troop ratio.
Do not send a bad first hero.
Keep marches returning quickly.
Join high-capacity rallies.
Avoid filling rallies with excess infantry when stronger damage troops are available.

17. Crazy Joe
Crazy Joe sends waves against alliance members and the Alliance HQ.
Typical structure:
Most waves attack member cities.
Key waves attack Alliance HQ.
Some waves require the player to be online.
Higher difficulty provides better rewards but can overwhelm weak alliances.
Individual strategy
Remove troops from your city by reinforcing alliance members when instructed.
Reinforce active, strong, or empty cities.
Coordinate hero use.
Recall troops for special waves when required.
Keep the city online for online-only waves.
Do not heal during the event unless alliance strategy calls for it, because healed troops can alter reinforcement scoring and capacity.
Alliance strategy
Publish wave instructions in advance.
Assign HQ reinforcement for the HQ waves.
Set minimum difficulty based on actual alliance strength.
Avoid overcrowding one city while others are empty.
Keep strongest defenders available for critical waves.
18. Alliance Championship
Alliance Championship uses three lanes.
Goal:
Win at least two lanes.
Strategy
Do not distribute power evenly by habit.
Scout opponent lane history when possible.
Stack two lanes to secure the match.
Use the third lane as a sacrifice when necessary.
Update formations after hero or troop upgrades.
Ensure every registered member deploys.
Assign members according to reliable combat strength, not visible power alone.
19. Foundry Battle
Foundry is an alliance battlefield focused on occupying buildings and collecting points.
Core principles
Occupation points win battles.
Eliminations matter mainly when they enable control.
Teleport discipline is crucial.
Reinforce important buildings.
Do not waste the whole match chasing weak cities.
Keep a reserve team for retakes.
Collect dropped Arsenal supplies or event resources when the mode provides them.
Follow command chat.
Suggested roles
Rally leads: attack heavily defended objectives.
Garrison leads: hold key buildings.
Fillers: reinforce and replace wounded marches.
Fast response: retake secondary buildings.
Gatherers: collect scattered resources.
Scouts/callers: track enemy movement and announce openings.
Common mistakes
Everyone attacks the central objective.
Empty captured buildings.
No reinforcement rotation.
No teleport plan.
Fighting far from objectives.
Ignoring point-generating secondary buildings.
Failing to withdraw before a bad hit.
